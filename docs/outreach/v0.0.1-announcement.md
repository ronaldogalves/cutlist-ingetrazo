## 2. Comment on ingelibre/ingetrazo#199

> **Cut List for IngeTrazo 0.0.1** is out as a pre-release:
> https://github.com/ronaldogalves/cutlist-ingetrazo/releases/tag/v0.0.1
>
> It lists the parts grouped by board and thickness. It reads materials
> face by face and edge by edge (laminates, veneers, edge bands) and
> exports through profiles you configure once per supplier. Profiles for
> **CorteCloud** (paste from Excel) and **Leo Madeiras** (its new web
> import) are included. Both suppliers' sites accepted the files, edge
> bands included. Feedback is welcome, and so are profiles for other
> suppliers.
>
> @antrrax, thank you for the test model. Our cut list matches your
> OpenCutList CSV part for part: the same 14 parts, sizes, quantities
> and edge bands. It also showed two things:
>
> - `panela_fechamento` is modelled 14 mm thick on a 15 mm board.
>   OpenCutList reports the board's 15; ours now flags the part.
> - On `.skp` import, groups and components do not stay apart. The 30
>   panel components arrive as plain groups, and the two walls
>   (`Agrupar#1`, `Agrupar#22`) arrive as components. So "components
>   only" cannot follow the SketchUp workflow you describe. I can open
>   a separate issue for this with the model as an example.
